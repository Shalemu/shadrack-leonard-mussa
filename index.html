Where beauty begins

Double H is an award-winning cosmetic brand that specialises in skin care solutions. Our products are recommended for stretch marks, uneven skin tone, oily, dehydrated & acne prone skin. Portia M started with just one product in 2011, we now have over 40 facial and body skin care products that are sold in over 2000 retail stores. Our History

✻

Double H is an award-winning cosmetic brand that specialises in skin care solutions. Our products are recommended for Stretch marks, Uneven Skin Tone, Oily, Dehydrated & Acne prone skin. Starting in 2011 with just one product, we now have 40+ facial and body skin care products available in over 2000 retail stores across Africa.

Our Vision

To become a leading skin care and cosmetics brand in Africa and globally, specialising in quality products created from locally sourced ingredients.

Integrity

We are fully committed to conducting business with integrity and to do right by our employees, customers, suppliers, partners and communities.

Quality
